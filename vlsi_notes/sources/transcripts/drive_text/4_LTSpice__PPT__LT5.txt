Level 1 Model (S-H Model)
• This model does not include subthreshold conduction or any short
channel effect such as mob. degradation
• Use GCA and square law approximations
• It has maintained the accuracy of I-V curve as small as for the
channel length of 4 µm.
• It predicts the output impedance of the transistor in saturation
quite poorly.
