NMOS Fabrication using the Metal Gate Process
These notes explain the fundamental steps involved in fabricating an NMOS (N-type Metal Oxide
Semiconductor) transistor on a wafer, focusing on the metal gate process.
Note: Please refer to the figures online.
I. Wafer Preparation and Contamination Control
1. Obtaining the Wafer: Silicon wafers are obtained from a single silicon crystal ingot through various
operations, including shaping, sawing, mechanical lapping, etching, and chemical mechanical polishing.
Wafers are fabricated to specific sizes, such as 125 mm, 150 mm, 200 mm, and 300 mm diameters,.
2. Basic Element: The basic element used to create Integrated Circuits (ICs) is the MOSFET (Metal
Oxide Semiconductor device).
3. The Need for Cleaning: Fabrication involves extensive mechanical and chemical processing, resulting
in contaminants potentially available on the wafer surface,.
If contaminants incorporate into the device, they will negatively affect device performance,.
Wafers must be repeatedly cleaned after virtually every step to ensure they are ultra-pure and
free from processing chemicals (like organic photoresist or etching solutions),.
II. Major Categories of Fabrication Steps
The foundational fabrication steps can be grouped into five major categories:
1. Wafer Preparation: The initial step of obtaining the single-crystal silicon wafer (e.g., using the
Czochralski technique, or Bridgman technique for Gallium Arsenide).
2. Oxide Formation: Growing oxide layers, often Silicon Dioxide (
), which can be done via dry
oxidation or wet oxidation techniques.
3. Patterning: The process of transferring a specific geometric pattern onto the wafer. This involves
photolithography, etching, photoresist, and a photo mask,.
4. Doping: Introducing impurities to create 
-doped or 
-doped regions, typically done using diffusion
or ion implantation techniques,.
5. Deposition: Applying layers (dielectric, organic, or metallic) onto the wafer surface using methods like
Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD), Sputtering, or Vacuum Deposition,.
Note: These steps are not always followed sequentially and may be used multiple times throughout the
fabrication process,.
III. Detailed Steps of NMOS Fabrication (Metal Gate Process)
The fabrication process begins with a P-type substrate and involves multiple oxidation, patterning, and
doping cycles.

1. Second Oxidation (Passivation): An
