Electronics Grade Silicon – Purification
- 2C(s) + SiO₂(s) → Si(l) + 2CO(g) (MSG Si 98% pure with Al and Fe as major impurities, reaction carried out at 2000°C)
- Si(s) + 3HCl(g) → SiHCl₃(g) + H₂(g) Explosive reaction. Trichlorosilane boiling point at 32°C.
- 2SiHCl₃(g) + 2H₂(g) → 2Si(s) + 6HCl(g) ESG silicon in the form of polysilicon nuggets.

Electronics Grade Silicon – Purification
1st step is metallurgical grade silicon (MGS) production in submerged electrode arc furnace.

The furnace is charged with quartzite and carbon. MGS is 98% pure. 90% of MGS goes into steel production.

ESG is produced from MGS in an arc furnace.

ESG is the raw material for the preparation of single crystal silicon – it is polycrystalline material of high purity and requires doping elements to be in parts per billion range.

Major impurities – boron, phosphorous, carbon.
