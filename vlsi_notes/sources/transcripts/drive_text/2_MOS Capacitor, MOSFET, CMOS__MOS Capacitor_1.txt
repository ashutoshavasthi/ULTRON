Introduction to the MOS Capacitor
I. The MOSFET and its Importance
The MOSFET (Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor) is recognized as the most common type
of transistor. MOSFETs are critical components that enable modern technology, including the processors
and memory devices found in your laptops, computers, and cell phones. Many modern electronic systems
rely on CMOS (Complementary MOS) devices, which are built upon MOSFET technology.
II. The Fundamental Building Block: The MOS Capacitor
To fully understand how a MOSFET works, we must first study its fundamental building block: the MOS
capacitor,.
1. MOS System Definition: The structure is called a Metal Oxide Semiconductor system (MOS).
2. Layers: It consists of three stacked layers: a semiconductor, an oxide (or dielectric) on top of the
semiconductor, and a metal on top of the oxide.
3. Capacitor Function: If you connect a metal, an oxide, and another metal, you have a flat-plate
capacitor. However, the MOS system is a capacitance system because it includes a metal, a
semiconductor, and an oxide layer in between.
