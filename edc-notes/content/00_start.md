## The five numbers that unlock half the paper

::: formula Memorise these first
$$V_T=\frac{T}{11600}\approx 26\ \text{mV at }300\text{ K}\qquad \eta=1\ (\text{Ge}),\ 2\ (\text{Si})$$
$$V_{\gamma}\ (\text{cut-in}):\ 0.3\text{ V (Ge)},\ 0.7\text{ V (Si)}\qquad I_0\ \text{doubles every }10^\circ\text{C}$$
$$V_{BE}\approx0.7\text{ V},\qquad I_C=\beta I_B,\qquad I_E=(\beta+1)I_B,\qquad r_e=\frac{26\text{ mV}}{I_E}$$
:::

## Study plan (for the days before the test)

::: remember Suggested order
1. **Day 1:** Ch. 1–3 and 11–13 (semiconductor physics and the diode). Do every "Try it yourself".
2. **Day 2:** Ch. 4–10 (special devices). These are mostly definitions, symbols, V–I curves and one or two formulas each, so make one flash-card per device.
3. **Day 3:** Ch. 14–15 (rectifiers, filters). Numericals live here. Redo the class problems without looking.
4. **Day 4:** Ch. 16–18 (BJT). Biasing formulas first, then AC. Solve each bias circuit once, then do its AC analysis.
5. **Last day:** Ch. 20 (formula sheet) and Ch. 21 (mock exam), timed.
:::
