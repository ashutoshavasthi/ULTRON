- **Noisy TV** (lesson 2): the lamp panel is pure randomness. Ultron gave it 16 of its 51 play choices (the minimum look every new thing gets), found no law, and stopped choosing it. Law for lamps: none (correct).

- **Designing experiments vs watching** (lessons 0-8, from `python -m ultron experiment`): experiences until the law it kept was first found, summed over all laws: 35 when Ultron designs experiments, 34 when it only watches random scenes. Per law where they differ (active, passive): {'earn_coins': (3, 4), 'earn_notes': (3, 6), 'get_paid': (5, 2), 'groups': (6, 3), 'pay': (3, 2), 'spend_notes': (3, 5)}. - **Composition, never trained**: a ball on a stretched spring. Ultron chained two laws it learned separately:

```
a = 2.4752 m/s²
  - stretch: F / x = 93.0092 for spring S_new  =>  F = 42.5982 N
  - push: F / (a·m) = 1  =>  a = 2.4752 m/s²
```
- **One-shot transfer** (real data): after seeing only Io, Ultron predicted Europa, Ganymede and Callisto: 3/3 (100%). A blank brain shown only Io: 0/3 (0%).
- The constant T²/r³ became a *property of the central body*. Jupiter's value is 1049× the Sun's. (Physicists know this ratio as the Sun-to-Jupiter mass ratio, ≈1047. Ultron was not told this.)
- **Noisy lab**: every reading was off by up to ±1%. Using error propagation from the instruments' stated precision, Ultron found the laws again and predicted the *true* values. Its lesson-5 laws steered the search: 281 candidates vs 348 for a blank brain (both succeeded; the saving is modest).
- **Inventing negative numbers** (lesson 8): nobody mentioned them. Ultron played with a purse of coins and IOU notes, then noticed: _My coins/notes states all lie on ONE line: 'earn' and 'spend' undo each other, and from the empty state 'earn' counts coins up 1, 2, 3... But 'spend' from the empty state doesn't stop: it keeps going, into states with notes. Those are places *below* zero. I'll treat them as numbers too, and use a new step 'down' that has no floor._ Only after that was it told the words '-3' and 'negative three'.
- **Tough final exam** (lesson 9): no teaching; kinds of question never practised. Division was never taught: Ultron answers 'what times 7 equals 84' by running its multiplication law backwards. For impossible questions the only correct answer is a refusal, and a guess counts as wrong.
